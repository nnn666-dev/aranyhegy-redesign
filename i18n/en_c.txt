In the shower room you will find quality toiletries, bathrobes, towels and hotel slippers, and a separate sheet is provided for the sauna.

s296 ::: Wellness and garden
s297 ::: Is the wellness area for our exclusive use?
s298 ::: The jacuzzi and the saunas are shared with the guests of the other apartment. Since there are never more than four guests in the house at once, using the shared areas is easy to coordinate. If both apartments have guests, we provide a 2-hour private evening wellness slot per couple on request, so your stargazing jacuzzi time is guaranteed to be yours alone. If you want to use it all day by yourselves, book the <strong>whole house</strong> (90,000 HUF / night, by phone), provided both apartments are free on those nights.
s299 ::: When can we use the jacuzzi and the saunas?
s300 ::: The covered jacuzzi, the Finnish sauna and the infrared sauna can be used undisturbed all year round, even in bad weather. Use is free and unlimited, and guides placed on site help you with the use. A sauna sheet is provided for the sauna. Aromatherapy oils are also available.
s301 ::: When can we use the pool?
s302 ::: The outdoor temperature-controlled pool is seasonal: it is open from spring to autumn. The jacuzzi and the sauna operate all year round.
s303 ::: What can we use in the garden?
s304 ::: The spacious 2,400 m² garden has sun loungers, a gazebo and a barbecue area. For the grill we set out charcoal and lighting supplies, and to explore the area we provide electric scooters free of charge. You will also find ideas about the area's sights on the information board in the apartment's entrance.
s305 ::: Can we charge an electric car at the property?
s306 ::: Yes, for an extra fee we also offer electric car charging. Charging costs 3,500 HUF per session, up to 50 kWh of energy. One vehicle can be charged at a time, and the owner of the vehicle provides the necessary cable. The service fee must be paid before check-out by online bank card payment or bank transfer.
