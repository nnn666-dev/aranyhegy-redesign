s176 ::: If you want to step out of the calm for a day, there is plenty to do around Szada: an arboretum, a royal palace, a bear sanctuary, an equestrian park and a basilica. All are within 30 minutes of the house by car.
s177 ::: Vácrátót · approx. 15 min
s178 ::: Vácrátót Arboretum
s179 ::: Hungary's largest botanical garden, with more than a century and a half of history.
s180 ::: The Vácrátót Arboretum is the country's largest botanical garden and has been running for more than a century and a half. Across its spacious, well-kept grounds, themed gardens, exotic and native plant collections, shady tree-lined avenues and picturesque lakes follow one another.
s181 ::: It shows a different face in every season: flowering bulbs and shrubs in spring, lush greenery and reflecting lakes in summer, golden foliage in autumn. You can stroll comfortably along wide, well-kept paths, and the greenhouses hold plants from all over the world.
s182 ::: A good choice for couples, families and solo visitors alike. It is excellent for quiet walks and photography, especially in autumn.

s184 ::: Veresegyház · approx. 15 min
s185 ::: Medveotthon Bear Sanctuary
s186 ::: A shelter and education centre for rescued brown bears and wolves.
s187 ::: The Medveotthon in Veresegyház works as a shelter and an education centre. Rescued brown bears and wolves live here in a spacious, natural habitat, in calm, stress-free surroundings, and visitors can watch them from a respectful distance.
s188 ::: The most popular experience is feeding the bears: with a long wooden spoon you can offer them honey. It is especially memorable for children, but adults also get a fascinating insight into the bears' behaviour and natural habits.
s189 ::: Comfortable footpaths and information boards help you find your way. It is an ideal family outing, mostly from spring to autumn, when the animals are more active. The visit also draws attention to the importance of animal welfare and responsible nature conservation.
s190 ::: Domonyvölgy · approx. 20 min
s191 ::: Domonyvölgy and Lázár Equestrian Park
s192 ::: Riding shows, carriage driving and traditional Hungarian food among rolling hills.
s193 ::: Lázár Equestrian Park lies among the rolling hills, green meadows and quiet countryside of Domonyvölgy. Here you can get to know Hungarian horse culture and rural traditions up close.
s194 ::: The park is known for its spectacular riding shows, where skilled riders present elements of traditional Hungarian riding and carriage driving. The programmes are often accompanied by live commentary, so you understand the significance of these centuries-old traditions even better.
s195 ::: On site there are restaurants serving traditional Hungarian dishes, a petting zoo you can walk through, and carriage rides. A good programme for families, groups and international guests, if you fancy a slower day close to nature.
s196 ::: Gödöllő · approx. 20 min
s197 ::: Gödöllő Arboretum (Arbo-Park)
s198 ::: A quiet forest park with accessible paths, also good for dog walking and picnics.
s199 ::: The Gödöllő Arboretum, known locally as Arbo-Park, is a quiet forest park close to nature. The well-kept, spacious grounds show a variety of forest types and many tree species, yet keep the calm of the natural setting. Come here if you want to get a little away from the bustle of the city.
s200 ::: On the well-kept, accessible paths, people of all ages can walk comfortably. It suits a family outing, dog walking or a quiet walk for two. Each season gives something different: fresh green leaves in spring and colourful foliage in autumn make the visit memorable.
s201 ::: Easy to walk, yet spacious enough to quieten down in. A good place for a picnic, light exercise or simply a slow walk in nature.
s202 ::: Gödöllő · approx. 10 min
s203 ::: Gödöllő Royal Palace
s204 ::: A baroque palace in Gödöllő, favourite summer residence of Queen Elisabeth (Sisi).
s205 ::: The Grassalkovich Palace in Gödöllő is one of Hungary's most important baroque monuments. It is especially well known because Queen Elisabeth, known as Sisi, used it as her favourite summer residence, and it also played an important role in the days of the Austro-Hungarian Monarchy.
s206 ::: In the splendidly furnished rooms, original furniture, works of art and historical exhibitions evoke everyday life at court. It is worth choosing a guided tour: it tells you in detail about the history of the palace, its residents and the political background of the era.
s207 ::: After your visit you can walk calmly in the park and ornamental gardens around the palace. An elegant, rewarding cultural programme for anyone interested in history, architecture and royal life.
s208 ::: Szada · approx. 25 min
s209 ::: Szada Ring
s210 ::: A 1,050-metre asphalt go-kart track with more than 15 karts.
s211 ::: Szada Ring is a modern go-kart track in Szada, on the far side of the village, so it is about 25 minutes from the house by car. The technical, 1,050-metre asphalt track is 8–10 metres wide, and the karts can reach speeds of up to 70 km/h in a safe environment.
s212 ::: Karts of different power mean beginners and more experienced drivers both have a good time. It is a good programme for groups of friends, families and corporate team-building alike: experienced staff help, races come with professional timing, and the vehicles are regularly maintained.
s213 ::: On site there is also a mini traffic park for the little ones and a bicycle pump track. For opening hours and booking, check the track's website.
s214 ::: Máriabesnyő · approx. 15 min
s215 ::: Máriabesnyő Basilica of the Assumption
s216 ::: Built in 1755, still a popular place of pilgrimage in Máriabesnyő.
s217 ::: The Máriabesnyő Basilica of the Assumption is one of Hungary's most important pilgrimage sites and has drawn visitors for more than two and a half centuries. The church, built in 1755, defines the skyline of the area from afar.
s218 ::: The interior is marked by richly decorated altars, finely detailed frescoes and harmonious proportions. The quiet of the church invites reflection, whether religious or cultural interest brought you here.
s219 ::: It is especially beautiful in the late afternoon, when the setting sun lights up the façade. A good short outing if you want calm and inner recharge.
s220 ::: Szada and surroundings
s221 ::: Hiking trails
s222 ::: Three marked hikes in the Gödöllő Hills: Margita tower, the Máriabesnyő loop, the Szada loop.
s223 ::: Margita and the survey tower with the Holy Crown
s224 ::: 3 km
s225 ::: 90 m ascent
s226 ::: approx. 1 hour
s227 ::: easy
s228 ::: approx. 5 min by car from the house
s229 ::: A short forest hike to Szada's highest point. On top of the 344.2-metre Margita stands a 30-metre geodetic survey tower built in 1974, with a stylised replica of the Holy Crown added in 2006. The marked path starts at the end of Arany János utca, at the edge of the village; at the junctions, the yellow stripe and red triangle markings lead to the tower.
s230 ::: From the top you can see the Visegrád Mountains, the Börzsöny, the Cserhát and the Mátra. Climbing the tower's inner ladders is dangerous, as a sign at the entrance says, so do it only at your own risk.
s231 ::: Trail description (Telex)
s232 ::: Máriabesnyő, Pap Miska Spring and the Babat church ruin
s233 ::: 12.4 km
s234 ::: 330 m ascent
s235 ::: approx. 4 hours
s236 ::: approx. 15 min by car from the house
s237 ::: The hike starts at the Székely gate of the Máriabesnyő shrine, past the Stations of the Cross and the basilica. The red marking climbs Gudra Hill (the hardest section, 65 metres of ascent, about a quarter of an hour), then descends on a narrow path to the Pap Miska Spring. From there you cross under the M3 motorway to the Babat stable manor and the memorial plaque of the church ruin, and return to Máriabesnyő along the Mária út markings.
s238 ::: Near the motorway, traffic noise accompanies you for a while. By car you can park in the car park next to the shrine. If you want something shorter: the walk to the Pap Miska Spring is 5.2 km, 150 m ascent, about 2 hours.
s239 ::: Trail description (Margita 344.2 TSE)
s240 ::: Szada loop: Magyalos-tető and Margita
s241 ::: 13.8 km
s242 ::: 316 m ascent
s243 ::: 3–5 hours
s244 ::: starts from the village
s245 ::: A marked loop from Szada through the forests of the Gödöllő Hills. The yellow cross marking leads out of the village, the yellow stripe takes you to Magyalos-tető, and from there the red stripe climbs to the top of Margita. On the way back you return to Szada on the familiar yellow stripe.
s246 ::: Shorter than a full-day outing, yet a rewarding hike for fitter walkers and families alike. According to the description, until a lookout tower is built, only the geodetic tower stands among the trees at the summit.
